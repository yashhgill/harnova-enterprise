## 27 Oct: Product showcase, AI Planter how it works (day17-showcase-ai-planter.png)
you don't need a green thumb. you need 2 numbers, checked 24/7 🌱

if you've ever grown anything, you know the routine. you guess. a bit more water today, the fan on when it feels hot, and you hope the plants agree.

we didn't want to guess. so our ESP32-S3 reads temperature and humidity around the clock and streams them to a FastAPI backend. when conditions change, relays switch the pump and fan on their own. then AI turns the readings into growing advice you'd actually follow, in English or Bahasa Malaysia, on an app that installs on any phone.

that last part mattered most to us. most IoT dashboards are made for engineers. ours is for the person holding the watering can.

it's called AI Planter. it won 1st place at Program SULAM, FTMK UTeM.

building an IoT project this semester? save this for your wiring plan ✦

## 28 Oct: Tip, 5 lines your GitHub README needs (day18-tip-github-readme.png)
5 lines your GitHub README needs before you send that job application.

when you apply, the repo link is the closest thing you have to a portfolio. and a lot of FYP repos open on the default README: the project name and nothing else.

so whoever clicks has to guess what it does, find out how to run it, and work out which part was yours. most people won't. they close the tab.

this takes about 15 minutes per repo. do your top 2 tonight and pin them to your profile.

## 31 Oct: Meme, 4 tech horror stories, Halloween (day20-meme-tech-horror.png)

## 2 Nov: Tip, 5 Google Maps fixes before your next customer searches (day22-tip-google-maps.png)
5 Google Maps fixes before your next customer searches for you.

think about how you pick a place to eat in a new area. you don't type a name. you search "coffee near me", look at 3 pins, and choose one before you've opened a single website.

your customers do the same thing to your shop. so the listing is doing the selling, and a lot of them were set up once and never touched again. old hours, 1 blurry photo, a phone number that rings out.

open your Business Profile tonight and check these 5:
1. the exact category. "café", not just "restaurant".
2. your hours, plus holiday hours.
3. real photos: the shop front, inside, your best product.
4. the call button and the website link. tap both yourself.
5. replies to your reviews, the bad ones first.

it's free and it takes about 10 minutes. the shop next door might not bother.

save this and do it before the weekend ✦
