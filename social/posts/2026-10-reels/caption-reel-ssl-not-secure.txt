someone finds your shop, likes the product, taps order. the checkout page loads and right next to your web address the browser says "Not secure", in grey, with a little warning icon.

they were about to type their name, phone number and address. now they're wondering if that's a good idea. most people don't message you to ask. they just close the tab.

that label usually means 1 thing: your site has no SSL certificate, so it loads on plain http instead of https. Chrome flags every page like that. On every phone, too.

the fix is often free:
1. check your hosting panel for "SSL" or "HTTPS". most hosts give you a free Let's Encrypt certificate, it's 1 toggle
2. turn on "force HTTPS" so old links without the s still redirect
3. open your site on your phone and look for the padlock instead of the warning
