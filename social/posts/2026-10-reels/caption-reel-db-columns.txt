14 migrations in and you're still renaming 1 database column 🗂️

it starts small. week 2 you call it name. week 4 the login screen wants username, so you add that. week 6 someone in the group adds user_name because they didn't see the first two. now 4 screens read 3 different columns and nobody knows which phone number is the real one.

the code isn't really the problem. the tables never got planned, so every new screen quietly reshapes them.

the fix takes 1 page and an afternoon, before any code:
1. list the nouns. every thing your app stores gets its own table.
2. draw who owns what. 1 user, many bookings means bookings gets a user_id.
3. walk through every screen and check it can get its data in 1 query.

renaming a box on paper costs nothing. renaming it in week 9 costs a migration and a weekend.
